# 70-hifi/usability/

> **What this is.** What happened when real people tried to do their job on the design.
>
> **Where it stands.** One round before the final internal review, then fixes, then a short
> re-run of anything that failed.
>
> **What we need from you.** Help us reach five people per role who do this work now.

| Produced by | Skill | Command | Checked at |
|---|---|---|---|
| design-critic | `usability-testing` | `/usability-round` | Gate 3 |

One file per round: `{round}-{date}.md`. It carries the participants by role, the tasks, the
task-by-participant grid with completion and time, the findings scored and routed, the verbatims,
and anything a participant said that contradicts the flows.

**The column to read first is "completed wrong":** someone who believes they succeeded and did
not. That is the defect that reaches production, gets repeated daily, and is found by an audit.

Where users could not be reached, the round runs with the domain expert instead, every finding is
marked as unverified by a real user, and the substitution is stated in the gate's first sentence.
That is a mitigation of the R-SME-SUB risk, not a satisfaction of it.
