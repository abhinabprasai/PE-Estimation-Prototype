# Solution Hypothesis Entry

> **What this is.** An idea someone in the room offered for how to solve a problem. We capture these and do not act on them yet, because the problem comes first.
>
> **Where it stands.** Parked until the requirements are settled.
>
> **What we need from you.** Nothing - we come back to these, and we will tell you what happened to each one.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | SH-{n} |
| Hypothesis | the solution as offered |
| Offered by | [PO] / [DA] / [TEAM:name] |
| Responds to | PS-IDs it addresses |
| Status | open / adopted-into REQ-{id} / parked (reason) / rejected (reason) |
| Notes | context from the call; verbatim where phrasing mattered |

Rule: nothing here is silently dropped; the offerer will ask. Reconciled in
refined-requirements, not at capture.

---

**Key.**
`[DA]` domain authority, the person who rules on how the business actually works - `[PO]` product owner - `[USER:role]` an end user in that role - `[TEAM]` someone on our team - `[DOC:section]` your own document - `[RESEARCH:source]` outside research - `[LLM]` inferred by a model, not verified by anyone.
`(confirmed)` the domain authority verified it, on record - `(asserted)` a human said it once - `(inferred)` nobody has said it.
