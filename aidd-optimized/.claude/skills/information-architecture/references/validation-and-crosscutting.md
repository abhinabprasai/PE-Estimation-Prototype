## Step 7: Validate the structure

Do not ship IA on assertion. Two cheap tests, both runnable with the DA and two or three real
users inside an hour.

**Tree test (findability).** Give the labels as a bare hierarchy, no interface. Ask "where would
you go to {task}?" for eight to twelve real tasks drawn from the to-be flows. Record first click
and final destination separately: a wrong first click that self-corrects is a labelling problem;
a wrong destination is a structural one. Anything under roughly 70 percent direct success on a
daily task needs rework before it is drawn.

**Reverse card sort (grouping).** Give people the leaf items and ask them to group and name the
groups. Their names are candidate labels, and their groupings expose where your entity-derived
structure disagrees with their mental model. Where the two disagree, the entity model usually
wins on structure and their words always win on labels.

Record both in `40-models/ia-validation.md` with participants, tasks, and results. On projects
where users are not reachable, run it with the DA and mark the results (asserted) rather than
skipping it.
